## 31 Want of omdat?
- **Grammar**:
  - Coordinating *want* — practiced — connects a main clause to another main clause; the clause after *want* keeps V2 order.
  - Subordinating *omdat* — practiced — sends the finite verb to the end of the short reason clause modeled here.
  - Fronted *omdat*-clause — practiced — take a comma, then invert the main clause (finite verb before subject).
