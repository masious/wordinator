## 35 Als ik tijd had
- **Grammar**:
  - Hypothetical *als* (“if”) vs temporal *als* (“when”) — practiced — hypothetical condition puts its finite verb at the end; temporal *als* describes a real or expected event.
  - *Zou/zouden* + infinitive — practiced — *zou* with singular subjects and polite *u*; *zouden* with plural subjects; the infinitive phrase goes at the end.
  - Optional *dan* after a fronted condition — basic — main-clause V2 remains (*Als …, dan zou ik …*).
