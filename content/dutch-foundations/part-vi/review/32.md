## 32 Wat is het leukst?
- **Grammar**:
  - Comparative + *dan*, and *even/net zo/niet zo … als* — practiced (recycled from 18-b) — compare with adjective + *-er* + *dan*; express equality with the base adjective + *als*.
  - Regular superlatives — practiced — predicative/adverbial forms commonly use *het* + *-st*; before a noun use its article + the inflected *-ste* form.
  - Superlative spelling — practiced — keep the adjective’s spelling pattern before *-st/-ste* (for example, *groot → grootst → grootste*).
  - Irregular comparison — basic/practiced — *goed → beter → best(e)* and *veel → meer → meest(e)*.
  - Preference — recycled — *graag → liever → het liefst*; *het liefst* expresses preference, not the regular superlative pattern.
