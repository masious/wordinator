| # | Lesson / communicative goal | Main grammar |
|---|---|---|
| **Part I — First Dutch** |
| 1 | Hello, goodbye, introducing yourself | **zijn**, personal pronouns, basic SVO |
| 2 | Where are you from? | **komen**, **wonen**, present tense singular |
| 3 | Languages & nationalities | regular present tense, **spreken**, verb position |
| 4 | Numbers, age & phone numbers | **hebben**, numbers, question words |
| 5 | Family & people | possessives: **mijn, jouw, zijn, haar**; **de/het** introduction |
| 6 | Describing people & things | adjectives; **de/het + adjective** basics |
| **Part II — Everyday life** |
| 7 | My daily routine | present tense plural; separable verbs introduction |
| 8 | Time & appointments | telling time; **om**, **van...tot**, inversion after time |
| 9 | Days, dates & schedules | word order with time expressions; **op/in** |
| 10 | Food & drink | plurals; **een/de/het**; countable quantities |
| 11 | At a café / restaurant | **willen**, **mogen**; polite requests |
| 12 | Shopping & prices | demonstratives: **deze/die/dit/dat** |
| **Part III — Home and surroundings** |
| 13 | My house | **er is / er zijn**, location vocabulary |
| 14 | Where is it? | prepositions of place; **staan/liggen/zitten** |
| 15 | My city & neighborhood | **kunnen**, directions, imperative |
| 16 | Transport | separable verbs in more depth; **gaan + infinitive** |
| 17 | Weather & seasons | impersonal **het**; adjective/adverb usage |
| 18 | Clothes | adjective endings; colors; comparison introduction |
| **Part IV — Doing things** |
| 19 | Hobbies & free time | **houden van**, **graag**, infinitive constructions |
| 20 | Making plans | modal verbs + infinitive; infinitive at sentence end |
| 21 | Invitations & arrangements | **zullen**, suggestions, requests |
| 22 | What are you doing? | **aan het + infinitive** |
| 23 | Ability, permission & obligation | **kunnen, mogen, moeten, hoeven** |
| 24 | Opinions & preferences | **vinden dat**, **denken dat**; subordinate clauses introduction |
| **Part V — Talking about the past** |
| 25 | What did you do yesterday? | perfect tense: **hebben + voltooid deelwoord** |
| 26 | Where did you go? | perfect with **zijn** |
| 27 | Past experiences | irregular participles; **ge-** rules |
| 28 | Childhood & past situations | simple past of **zijn, hebben**, common verbs |
| 29 | Telling a story | perfect vs simple past; sequencing words |
| **Part VI — More complex Dutch** |
| 30 | Work & study | subordinate clauses: **omdat, dat, als** |
| 31 | Giving reasons | **want vs omdat**, clause word order |
| 32 | Comparing things | comparative & superlative |
| 33 | People and things | relative clauses: **die/dat** |
| 34 | Explaining purpose | **om ... te**, **te + infinitive** |
| 35 | Conditions & possibilities | **als**, **dan**, conditional-style **zou/zouden** |
| 36 | Future plans | present vs **gaan** vs **zullen** |
| **Part VII — Functional independence** |
| 37 | Problems & asking for help | object pronouns; polite conditional **zou** |
| 38 | Doctor, municipality, services | reflexive verbs; formal requests |
| 39 | Messages, emails & conversations | conjunctions; clause linking; register |
| 40 | Telling your story | integrated word order: main + subordinate clauses |
