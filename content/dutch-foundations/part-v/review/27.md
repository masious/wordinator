## 27 Wat heb je gezien en gedaan?
- **Grammar**:
  - Perfect with *hebben* and *zijn* — practiced — use the already-taught finite helper and put the participle at the clause end; yes/no questions put the helper first.
  - Common irregular participles — intro — learn *gedaan, gezien, gegeten, gedronken, gelezen, geschreven, gehad,* and *geweest* as whole forms.
  - Regular participle review — recycled — *ge-* + stem + *-t/-d*.
  - Inseparable prefixes *be-*, *ver-* and *ont-* — intro — no *ge-* in the participle.
  - Verbs ending in *-eren* — intro — add one *ge-* before the stem (*studeren → gestudeerd*).
  - Separable verbs — recycled — place *ge* between the prefix and verb (*opbellen → opgebeld*).
  - Participle-final word order and question inversion — practiced — keep the participle at the end; put the finite helper before the subject in yes/no questions.
- **Vocabulary** (21 words, 4 blocks): *de ervaring* (de ervaringen); *de smaak* (de smaken); *de brief* (de brieven); *zien*; *eten*; *drinken*; *doen*; *lezen*; *schrijven*; *hebben*; *zijn*; *bezoeken*; *vertellen*; *ontdekken*; *studeren*; *het verhaal* (de verhalen); *het voorvoegsel* (de voorvoegsels); *onscheidbaar*; *scheidbaar*; *bekijken*; *opschrijven*. Most verb infinitives are familiar; the cards teach their new participles and participle patterns. Recycled infinitives have notes on the cards where applicable.
- **Expressions**: *Heb je … gezien?*; *Wat heb je … gedaan?*; *Met kerst …*; *Ik heb … gehad.*
- **Story facts**: On Wednesday 6 January 2027, Sofia and Noor continue their catch-up at home. They look at housewarming photos and recall Christmas activities in Valencia: Sofia cooked and ate with her mother, studied Dutch, read a book, and wrote to her mother. They drink tea; Noor called Sofia.
- **Deviations from brief**: None material. The lesson stays with perfect forms and participle spelling; it adds no dated trip or simple-past/tense-comparison instruction.
- **Review changes**:
  - Replaced “De paella ziet er goed uit” with “De paella is lekker” to avoid introducing the unscoped *er goed uitzien* construction.
  - Clarified the *-eren* pattern as taking one *ge-* before the stem.
  - Corrected the description of *bekijken*: it has an inseparable *be-* prefix and the irregular vowel-changing participle *bekeken*; it is not a regular participle.
  - Added concise rule notes to practice items that tested irregular participles or perfect-tense word order.
- **Open questions for the user**: None.
