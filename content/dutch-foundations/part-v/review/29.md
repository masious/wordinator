## 29 Eerst, toen en uiteindelijk
- **Grammar**:
  - Perfect vs simple past — practiced — the perfect is common for completed events in conversation; the simple past is common for past states/background and connected accounts; these are tendencies, not absolute rules, and either may fit a different context.
  - Sequencing words — practiced — `eerst`, `toen`, `daarna`, and `uiteindelijk` show event order; `toen` is used as a time word before a main clause.
  - Fronted time expressions and V2 — practiced — a fronted time word takes the first position; the finite verb follows before the subject, while the participle stays at the end.
