- **Grammar**:
  - *want* vs. *omdat* — practiced — *want* joins main clauses with V2; *omdat* introduces a clause with the finite verb at the end.
  - Subordinate clauses with *dat* and *als* — practiced — the finite verb goes at the end; a fronted *als*-clause is followed by a comma and main-clause inversion.
