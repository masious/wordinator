## 21-b Ik haal je op
- **Grammar**:
  - Object pronouns for people — practiced — subject forms map to object forms: ik → me/mij, jij/je → je/jou, u → u, hij → hem, zij/ze (she) → ze/haar, wij/we → ons, jullie → jullie, zij/ze (they) → ze; me/je/ze are lighter, mij/jou/haar are stressed; hen/hun are deferred.
  - Object pronouns for things — practiced — de-word → hem, singular het-word → het, plural → ze.
  - Object pronoun position — practiced — after the finite verb and, in inversion, after the subject; before time/place and separable prefix; after a modal and subject but before the final infinitive.
  - Person pronouns after prepositions — basic — use forms such as met mij, voor jou, bij ons; repeat a thing noun rather than using an er-form.
  - Invitations and arrangements — practiced — uitnodigen separates; accept, decline, ask for a reply, and arrange a pickup with a person object.
- **Vocabulary**: 21 words in 7 blocks — de housewarming; uitnodigen; de uitnodiging; het adres; appen; Ik app je.; Laat je het even weten?; Ik kom graag!; me / mij; je / jou; u; hem; haar; ons; jullie; ze; brengen; de bel (new doorbell sense; bicycle-bell sense known from 16-b); aanbellen; Helaas kan ik niet.; Ik zie je zaterdag. (known chunk from 07-a, now explained for object je).
- **Expressions**: Kom je ook?; Ja, natuurlijk!; Ik kom graag!; Laat je het even weten?; Helaas kan ik niet.; Ik haal … op.; Ik nodig … uit.; Ken je …?; Bel me maar!; Ik app je.; Ik zie je zaterdag.; Tot zaterdag!
- **Story facts**: On Monday 30 November, Sofia invites friends to a housewarming at Kastanjestraat 14 on Saturday 12 December at 19.00. Henk, Noor, Fleur, Daan, Lotte, Piotr and Kasia accept; Ahmed cannot come because he is with his family. Daan picks up Lotte. Sofia sends the address to Piotr and arranges to meet him and Kasia at the Lombok bus stop at 18.30, then take them to the party by bike.
- **Deviations from brief**: None material. The lesson has two dialogues (18 turns total), 21 examples, 8 practice blocks and 53 practice items. It includes the brief’s invitation, pickup, and housewarming arrangements.
- **Review changes**: Replaced the prohibited “Zonder haar is het adres moeilijk te vinden” with “Zonder haar weet ik het adres niet.” Completed the object-pronoun table with u and distinguished unstressed ze (her) from ze (them). Split the vocabulary list into 7 cards placed alongside first use; added the doorbell words to the invitation section and a matching example for “Ik zie je zaterdag.”
