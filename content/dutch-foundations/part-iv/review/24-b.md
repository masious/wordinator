## 24-b Ik denk dat het gaat sneeuwen
- **Grammar**:
  - Main clause + *dat*-clause — practiced — *vinden dat* gives an opinion, *denken dat* a belief or expectation, *hopen dat* a hope, and *zeggen dat* a report; no comma before *dat*.
  - Word order after *dat* — practiced — keep the subject after *dat* without inversion; put the conjugated verb at the end. In a two-verb cluster, the conjugated verb comes before the infinitive; separable verbs join at the end.
  - *Niet* in a *dat*-clause — practiced — place it before the final verb or verb cluster; *niet* before *dat* negates the opinion in the main clause.
  - Short answers — basic — *Ik denk het wel* stays a main clause without *dat*.
- **Vocabulary**: 15 words in 4 blocks — *dat* (conjunction; demonstrative known from 12-a); direct; Pools; allemaal (new use for a group; greeting known from 08-b); hopen; *Fijne kerst!*; de oliebol; het vuurwerk; de wens; *Fijne feestdagen!*; *Prettige kerstdagen!*; de oudejaarsavond; de kerstgroet; *Gelukkig nieuwjaar!*; *Tot volgend jaar!*.
- **Expressions**: *Ik denk dat …*; *Ik vind dat …*; *Ik hoop dat …*; *Marieke zegt dat …*; *Ik denk het wel*; *Fijne feestdagen!*; *Prettige kerstdagen!*; *Fijne kerst!*; *Gelukkig nieuwjaar!*; *Goede reis!*; *Tot volgend jaar!*.
- **Story facts**: At the last Dutch class on Tuesday 15 December, Marieke says she hopes everyone returns in January; Piotr reports that Marieke says Sofia speaks good Dutch. On Friday 18 December, Sofia writes to her mother before flying from Schiphol to Valencia at 10.00 the next morning. She says Utrecht is really her city now, expects to miss her bike, and returns to Utrecht on Sunday 3 January 2027. Henk and Tijger say goodbye to her in the hallway; Henk expects snow at Christmas.
- **Deviations from brief**: No material deviations. The lesson adds the flight departure time (10.00) and Marieke's praise of Sofia; neither conflicts with the established timeline or story.
