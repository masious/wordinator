## 20-b Donderdag kan ik niet
- **Grammar**:
  - Position of *niet* — practiced — at the end when nothing follows; after definite objects, names, time words and short adverbs; before adjectives, adverbs, prepositional phrases, infinitives, separable prefixes and *nodig*.
  - *geen* — practiced — replaces *niet + een* and negates nouns without an article; definite nouns take *niet*.
  - *niet … maar …* — practiced — corrects a choice; place *niet* before the corrected element.
  - *wel* — practiced — gives positive contrast to an earlier negative.
  - Fronting and inversion — practiced — one element before the finite verb, followed by the subject.
- **Vocabulary**: 18 words in three blocks: de bioscoop, de voorstelling, kennen, misschien, nog niet, ook niet, Niet …, maar …, Dat komt goed., de fout, de knop, de test, de computer, opnieuw, werken (new meaning: to function), doen (informal: to function), Gelukkig!, het programma, draaien (new meaning: a film is showing).
- **Expressions**: *Donderdag kan ik niet*; *Dat komt goed*; *Gelukkig!*; *Niet …, maar …*; *nog niet*; *ook niet*.
- **Story facts**: Noor attends a friend's party on Friday; Noor, Sofia and Fleur plan dinner at Noor's on Saturday; Noor and Sofia choose the Sunday 29 November, 15.00 showing of *De Lange Zomer* in the centre, and Noor buys the tickets. Sofia and Daan work late together on Thursday 26 November; the app test eventually works.
