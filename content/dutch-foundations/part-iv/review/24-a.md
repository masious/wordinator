## 24-a Wat vind jij ervan?
- **Grammar**:
  - *vinden + object + adjective* — practiced — put the object after *vinden* and the describing adjective after the object; recycle *hem* for a de-word, *het* for an het-word, and *ze* for a plural.
  - Asking for an opinion — practiced — use *Wat vind je van …?* with a noun and the fixed chunk *Wat vind jij ervan?*; *ervan* is not taught as a general er-form system.
  - *vinden* vs *denken* — practiced — use *vinden* for a personal opinion or judgement and *denken* for a belief or expectation; use *Ik denk het wel/niet* and *Ik vind van wel/niet* as short answers.
  - Agreeing and disagreeing — practiced — use *Ik ben het (niet) met … eens*, *Je hebt gelijk*, and *Dat vind ik ook*.
  - *Volgens mij* + inversion — practiced — when *Volgens mij* starts the sentence, put the finite verb next and the subject after it.
  - Opinion adjectives — practiced — *lekker, vies, raar, gezellig* and other known adjectives can follow the object.
