# What is Wordinator?

Wordinator is a private, self-hosted social space for friends learning a language together. Members already learn from different books, lessons, websites, and daily experiences. Wordinator gives them one calm place to turn what they encountered into small pieces of practice for one another.

The working title **Wordinator** may change.

## Who it is for

The initial community is roughly ten existing friends. It is not a public network, professional course provider, tutoring marketplace, or native-speaker matching service. Trust comes from real relationships and invite-only groups.

Each group focuses on exactly one target language. The initial choices are Dutch and German. A person may belong to several groups, but each group is a separate social and data boundary.

## The core loop

1. A member learns a sentence, encounters a useful question, or prepares a short exercise.
2. They publish it to the group’s newest-first feed.
3. Friends answer without accidentally seeing earlier responses.
4. The group discusses, reacts, and optionally highlights one useful answer.
5. Everyone returns as new material is shared.

Posts are shared sentences, standalone questions, readings with questions, or fill-in-the-blank prompts. Content comes from members; the platform does not teach a curriculum or judge people’s learning.

Members may also build shared, member-authored [courses](courses.md) for their group.

## Product character

Wordinator should feel like a cozy shared study journal: modern, warm, tactile, quietly playful, and personal. It favors thoughtful participation over points, streaks, rankings, and attention-maximizing mechanics.

## Why build it

Existing language-exchange and writing-correction products provide public feeds, partner matching, or native-speaker feedback. Group chat provides intimacy but not structured exercises or spoiler-safe answers. Wordinator combines a private friend group, a focused chronological feed, and lightweight member-authored activities.

## Business context

This is a private, non-commercial project. There is no current plan for monetization, a hosted service, or open-source distribution. “Business” success means usefulness: approximately ten friends use it daily for several weeks and prefer it to sharing learning material in general-purpose chats.

The expected load is small—about ten users and up to one hundred new posts per day—so clarity and maintainability matter more than distributed-system scale.

## Explicit non-goals

- Public discovery, public profiles, followers, or direct messages
- Platform-provided lessons or a fixed curriculum, translation, dictionaries, grammar correction, pronunciation, or AI generation
- Scores, streaks, badges, leaderboards, or other gamification
- Automated language detection or enforcement
- Rich text, media posts, audio, or file attachments
- Monetization, advertising, or product analytics
