## 33 De collega die hier werkt (accepted 2026-10-19)
- **Grammar**: subject relative clauses with *die/dat*, finite verb at the end; distinguishes relative *dat* from conjunction *dat*.
- **Vocabulary** (15): *de omschrijving, het portret, het gezicht, de bril, krullend, blond, het kapsel, herkennen, aanwijzen, de afbeelding, het voorwerp, hetzelfde, bedoelen, naast elkaar,* and *ernaast*.
- **Story facts**: Thursday 14 January; at Karin's office, she shows Sofia a photo of Daan and a building picture. Sofia recognizes the building; Daan later helps her identify a card. No new character or backstory.
- **Review log**: [33.md](../../content/dutch-foundations/part-vi/review/33.md)
- **Production import**: 2026-10-19, unpublished draft at position 3 (9 practice anchors).

## 34 Om te studeren (accepted 2026-10-19)
- **Grammar**: purpose *om … te + infinitive*, including separable *op te zoeken* and *mee te nemen*; introduces *proberen te* and recycles *hoeven … te* and positional verb + *te*.
- **Vocabulary** (18): *het woordenboek, de betekenis, de uitspraak, de aantekening, de klank, onthouden, het doel, de oefening, de bladzijde, opzoeken, handig, de woordenlijst, de concentratie, de afleiding, de telefoon wegleggen, de markeerstift, markeren,* and *de pauzeknop*.
- **Story facts**: Friday 15 January; Sofia and Noor study Dutch together using a dictionary, word list and notes. No new appointment, schedule or work detail.
- **Review log**: [34.md](../../content/dutch-foundations/part-vi/review/34.md)
- **Production import**: 2026-10-19, unpublished draft at position 4 (9 practice anchors).

## 35 Als ik tijd had (accepted 2026-10-19)
- **Grammar**: hypothetical *als* with clause-final finite verb, contrasted with temporal *als*; *zou/zouden + infinitive*; optional *dan* with main-clause V2.
- **Vocabulary** (15): *de optie, het alternatief, de voorwaarde, de mogelijkheid, de kans, beschikbaar, haalbaar, overwegen, besluiten, de beslissing, het voordeel, het nadeel, de voorkeur, nadenken over,* and *de combinatie*.
- **Story facts**: Monday 18 January; Sofia and Noor discuss explicitly imaginary choices in Utrecht and Valencia, establishing no real appointment, plan or preference.
- **Review log**: [35.md](../../content/dutch-foundations/part-vi/review/35.md)
- **Production import**: 2026-10-19, unpublished draft at position 5 (9 practice anchors).

## 36 Wat gaan we morgen doen? (accepted 2026-10-19)
- **Grammar**: present tense + future time for timetables/settled arrangements; *gaan + infinitive* for intentions; *zullen + infinitive* for expectations, promises and offers. Recycles suggestions from 21-a and time-first V2.
- **Vocabulary** (15): *het rooster, het tijdstip, bijna afgelopen, de verwachting, zeker weten, het weerbericht, regenachtig, de starttijd, de eindtijd, de vaste tijd, waarschijnlijk, beloven, het aanbod, delen,* and *de voorspelling*.
- **Story facts**: Tuesday 19 January, after the regular Dutch class, Sofia and Piotr review the next Tuesday class time and discuss ordinary next-day study plans and uncertain weather. No new appointment or work schedule is established.
- **Review log**: [36.md](../../content/dutch-foundations/part-vi/review/36.md)
- **Production import**: 2026-10-19, unpublished draft at position 6 (8 practice anchors). Read-only verification confirmed all seven Part VI lessons at positions 0–6, all unpublished, and the course remains draft.

## 30 Omdat ik werk (accepted 2026-10-19)
- **Grammar**: recycles *dat* after *denken, vinden, hopen,* and *zeggen*; introduces *omdat* for a reason and temporal *als* for repeated/expected “when”; verb-final subordinate clauses; fronted subordinate clause followed by comma and main-clause inversion.
- **Vocabulary** (17 words in 3 blocks): *de bijzin, de hoofdzin, de reden, de werkplek, overleggen, duidelijk, begrijpen, de uitleg, de tekst, vragen aan, het onderwerp, de komma, aandachtig, de zin, het antwoord, hardop, het overleg*. *Verder* is recycled from 20-a, not a new card.
- **Story facts**: Monday 11 January, Sofia studies a Dutch text at the IT office and discusses it with Daan and Karin. No new project, deadline, study result or schedule is established.
- **Review log**: [30.md](../../content/dutch-foundations/part-vi/review/30.md)
- **Production import**: 2026-10-19, unpublished draft at position 0 (9 practice anchors).

## 31 Want of omdat? (accepted 2026-10-19)
- **Grammar**: *want* connects main clauses and preserves V2; *omdat* subordinates and puts the finite verb at the end; a fronted *omdat*-clause triggers main-clause inversion.
- **Vocabulary** (15 new words/chunks): *de opdracht, het bord* (classroom board; new sense), *vooraan, het voorbeeld, snappen, Vraag gerust!, uitleggen, de woordvolgorde, het verschil, voor de komma, na de komma, de laatste zin, lastig, extra, om uitleg vragen*.
- **Story facts**: Tuesday 12 January, Sofia and Piotr attend Marieke's 19.00 Dutch class at the library in the centre; they discuss where to sit and ask for help with a sentence.
- **Review log**: [31.md](../../content/dutch-foundations/part-vi/review/31.md)
- **Production import**: 2026-10-19, unpublished draft at position 1 (9 practice anchors).

## 32 Wat is het leukst? (accepted 2026-10-19)
- **Grammar**: recycles the comparative and equality patterns from 18-b; introduces regular *-st/-ste* superlatives, predicative/adverbial *het + -st*, spelling, and *best/beter* and *meest/meer*. *Het liefst* is recycled as a preference expression, not a regular superlative.
- **Vocabulary** (15 words in 4 blocks): *helder, donker, smal, breed, populair, eenvoudig, ingewikkeld, verrassend, comfortabel, aantrekkelijk, rommelig, netjes, modern, ouderwets,* and *de superlatief*. *Donker* is carded as a standalone adjective; its earlier appearance was only in color compounds.
- **Story facts**: Wednesday 13 January, Sofia and Noor compare established city, food and activity facts. No new fixed story facts or preferences were added.
- **Review log**: [32.md](../../content/dutch-foundations/part-vi/review/32.md)
- **Production import**: 2026-10-19, unpublished draft at position 2 (8 practice anchors). Read-only verification confirmed positions 0–2, all unpublished, and the course remains draft.
