# Dutch Foundations — storyline and cast

Owner: content coordinator. Part of the [content docs](index.md). Every dialogue, reading passage and example in Parts II–III follows this file.


## Setting

Utrecht, the Netherlands, from September to November 2026. The year matters for dates: **di 1 september 2026** is a Tuesday, **za 19 september** a Saturday, **za 17 oktober** a Saturday, **za 14 november** a Saturday.

Sofia Moreno, a young Spanish software developer, arrives in Utrecht for a new job. Part II follows her first weeks of everyday life while she rents a temporary room in her friend Noor's flat. The room is only free until **31 oktober** (Noor's sister Fleur comes back from Australia on 1 november), so Sofia must find her own place. At the Saturday market (row 12) the cheese seller Henk tells her that the apartment above his is for rent. Part III row 13 (existing lessons) is that apartment's listing: "Licht appartement met balkon", second floor, 72 m², big living room, small kitchen, two bedrooms, bathroom with shower, small sunny balcony, no garden, no garage, a lift, close to the centre. Part III follows her moving in (row 14), discovering the neighbourhood of Lombok (15), getting around (16), her first Dutch autumn (17), and buying winter clothes (18).

Places (keep consistent):
- **Noors appartement**: a small flat in the east of Utrecht (Utrecht-Oost). Sofia has one room ("mijn kamer"); they share the kitchen and the bathroom.
- **Sofia's work**: a small IT company ("een klein IT-bedrijf") with an office ("het kantoor") near Utrecht Centraal. Work starts at nine.
- **Café De Zwaluw**: fictional café on the Oudegracht where Noor works on Saturdays.
- **De markt op het Vredenburg**: the real Saturday market in the centre. Henk's cheese stall ("de kaaskraam") is there.
- **Kastanjestraat 14**: Sofia's new apartment, fictional street in the real neighbourhood **Lombok**, west of the centre. Henk lives on the first floor ("de eerste verdieping"), Sofia on the second ("de tweede verdieping"). The building has a lift. Nearby: the Kanaalstraat with many shops, a bakery, a supermarket, a pharmacy, the library, a park, the big mosque, Ahmed's bike shop, a tram/bus stop. Utrecht Centraal is about 15 minutes' walk.
